Borrow

You can take one book home if you come while the room is open.

  1. Come on Saturday between 9:00 and 13:00.
  2. Check the loan board so you do not choose a book that is already out.
  3. Take one book from the shelves.
  4. Write your name beside the title on the loan board.
  5. Bring the book back on the next Saturday.

Open Saturday, 9:00 to 13:00. One book for one week.
